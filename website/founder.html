Meet the Founder

Daksh

Founder & Lead Developer, Genatis

"I didn't start Genatis to build a business. I started it because my teachers deserved better tools — and nobody was building them."

✉ Email LinkedIn Twitter

🧑‍💻

Who I am

I'm Daksh — a high school student, self-taught developer, and the person behind Genatis. I write code, design interfaces, and build products that solve real problems in education.

I started coding in middle school — first with HTML pages, then Python scripts, then full-stack apps. But the project that changed everything was the one I built for my own school.

💡

Why I built Genatis

Every morning, I watched my teachers spend the first 10 minutes of class flipping through attendance registers, checking timetable printouts, and chasing students for homework diaries. It was chaos.

Teachers were drowning in paperwork instead of teaching. Principals had no real-time data. Parents had no visibility. And students lost precious learning time every day.

I thought: "What if there was one app — fast, clean, reliable — that replaced all of this?"

"The best technology is the kind that disappears — it works so well that you forget it's there."

— My design philosophy

🛤️

The journey

2024

The first version

Built a basic timetable manager as a desktop app. Teachers loved the concept but wanted more.

2025

Attendance & analytics

Added one-tap attendance, visual analytics, parent notifications, and a desktop widget.

2025

Parent portal & mobile app

Built a full parent portal with real-time chat, notices, homework submissions, and a mobile app.

2026

Genatis is born

Rebranded, redesigned, and launched as a platform for schools across India.

🏫

My school experience

Being a student while building a school management platform gives me a unique advantage: I live in the problem. I sit in the classroom. I see what works and what doesn't. I hear the complaints.

My teachers became my first beta testers. My classmates became my focus group. My principal became my first critic. Every feature in Genatis was born from a real pain point I witnessed firsthand.

That's why Genatis doesn't feel like "enterprise software." It feels like something a teacher would actually want to use.

Philosophy

How I build products

Three principles that guide every decision.

⚡

Speed is respect

Every second a teacher waits is a second stolen from students. Genatis must feel instant.

🎨

Design is function

Good design makes complex tasks feel simple. If it looks confusing, it is confusing.

🤝

Build with, not for

Every feature is co-created with teachers. I don't assume — I listen, observe, and iterate.

🌍

My vision for education

I believe every school in India — whether it's a small-town primary school or a city institution — deserves access to thoughtful management tools. Not clunky enterprise software, but clean, fast technology that respects the people who use it.

Genatis is just the beginning. My mission is to build a world where teachers focus entirely on teaching, parents stay connected, and students get the attention they deserve.
